small team. serious standards.

things we try to do

  • Start from the evidenceYour data and your constraints, before any code.
  • Show our workingResults you can explain to your team and your users.
  • Ship something real, earlyA working prototype beats a long slide deck.

things we try to avoid

  • Black boxes nobody can defendIf we can't explain it, we don't ship it.
  • Jargon for its own sakePlain language, in English or Marathi.
  • Hype over yieldDoes it help? That is the only test.
