Founder

Kapil Wankhede

Kapil started his career as a Quantitative Financial Analyst in New York, working on model risk management after an MS in Finance at Illinois Tech. He returned to India in 2018 to help build Nalanda Academy in Wardha, supporting students from rural, tribal, nomadic and migrant communities, and he still teaches mathematics himself.

Why EYP? The name stands for Evidence, Yield, Prediction: decisions grounded in evidence, judged by the yield they deliver, and guided by prediction.

EYP Labs brings that mix together: financial-grade rigor, applied machine learning and a deep respect for the people who use the tools.
